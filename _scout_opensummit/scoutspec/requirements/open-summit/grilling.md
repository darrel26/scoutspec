# Grilling Log: Open Summit

## Round 1

### Question 1: Participant Vetting Rigor
- **Question**: How strictly should Open Summit gate participant join requests for mountain summit trips?
- **Selection**: Organizer Approval
- **Rationale**: Organizers manually review attendee profiles, past summit history, and gear checklists before approving join requests, balancing group safety with community control.

### Question 2: In-App Communication Depth
- **Question**: What level of trip communication should be built natively into Open Summit for MVP?
- **Selection**: Native Chat + Pin
- **Rationale**: Built-in group chat with pinned announcements ensures critical details (meetup time, weather pivots, gear changes) don't get lost.

### Question 3: Carpool & Logistics Model
- **Question**: How should carpooling and driver/passenger logistics be handled in MVP?
- **Selection**: De-scoped for MVP
- **Rationale**: Carpool management de-scoped from MVP to keep initial release lean; coordination handled via native trip chat.
